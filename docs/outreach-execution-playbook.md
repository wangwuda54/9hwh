# Outreach Execution Playbook

## Stage 17 Goal

- Start the outreach ledger now, not later.
- Keep all first-week `owned_profile` tasks in `pending` until a real link is placed.
- Do not fabricate `actual_url`.

## Logging Rules

- Leave `actual_url` blank until a real profile link is live.
- Keep `status` as `pending` until the task is actually placed by a human.
- Use `submitted`, `live`, `rejected`, or `skipped` only after real execution.
- Record `nofollow` as `true`, `false`, or `unknown` only when checked.
- Use `notes` to capture operator remarks and blockers.

## What Not To Do

- Do not buy spam links.
- Do not run PBNs.
- Do not submit to bulk directories.
- Do not use automated comments.
- Do not invent owned profile URLs before they exist.

## Week 2+ Direction

- Move to real community answers and partner/resource opportunities only after the 15 `owned_profile` items are handled.
- Prioritize relevance and real brand visibility over link volume.
