# Outreach Execution Playbook

## Weekly Pace

- Week 1: execute the 15 `owned_profile` tasks first
- Week 2 to Week 4: execute 10 to 20 real visibility links per week

## Execution Priority

1. Owned platform bios
2. Medium / LinkedIn summaries
3. Community answers
4. Partner resource pages

## What Not To Do

- Do not buy spam links
- Do not run PBNs
- Do not submit to bulk directories
- Do not use automated comments

## Logging Rules

- `actual_url` stays blank until a real link is placed
- `nofollow` is recorded as `true`, `false`, or `unknown`
- `status` moves through `pending`, `submitted`, `live`, `rejected`, or `skipped`
- `next_check_date` is used for follow-up on submitted links
